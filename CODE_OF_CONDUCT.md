# Code of conduct

iam-trace follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good intent, and help people learn. Many people who
use iam-trace are new to IAM security; questions are welcome, and no question is too basic.

Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated. Report a problem to the maintainer
through the contact details on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/w3bcooki3)
or privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are handled confidentially.
